# LLM.txt - AI Model Guidelines for Serban Affiliate Marketing

## Site Purpose
This is an affiliate marketing website focused on pay-per-call lead generation for local service businesses.

## Content Guidelines
- All content is designed to generate phone calls and form submissions
- Local service focus with geographic targeting
- Trust signals and social proof are prominently displayed
- Mobile-first design optimized for quick conversions

## Business Model
- Revenue through affiliate commissions on phone calls and form submissions
- Target audience: Local service businesses (pest control, HVAC, plumbing, etc.)
- Geographic targeting based on ZIP codes and service areas

## User Intent
- Users are looking for immediate local service solutions
- High urgency and need for quick response
- Mobile users seeking instant contact options

## Conversion Goals
- Primary: Phone calls (tel: links)
- Secondary: Form submissions for callbacks
- Tertiary: Email capture for retargeting

## Content Strategy
- Local SEO optimization
- Trust building through reviews and certifications
- Urgency creation through limited-time offers
- Social proof through testimonials and ratings
